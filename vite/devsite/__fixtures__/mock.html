HttpTransport

A class in @seikou/engine.

class HttpTransport implements RangeTransport

Reads <base>files/<path>?t=<token> with a single Range: bytes=a-b and no other headers, so the request needs no preflight. base ends with /: https://…/bluray/api/discs/<id>/. A 503 is waited out for its Retry-After, and a 401 is retried once with a fresh token. Runs wherever it is built: the engine worker or the BD-J worker.
